# DUMBER Promo — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

A seductive, hypnotic product film that never leaves a native HTML/CSS reconstruction of the X feed. The feed is the world, not a screenshot placed inside a poster. Iridescence stays attached to meaningful surfaces: the DUMBER scanner orb, the selected-card perimeter, emotional words, and finally the wordmark underline. The experience should feel frictionless, luxurious, slightly funny, and completely sincere about making scrolling more pleasurable.

## Colors

- `#050509` — canvas black
- `#00F5FF` — electric cyan / scan lead
- `#665CFF` — depth blue
- `#B64CFF` — violet bridge
- `#FF2E88` — dopamine pink
- `#FFD45C` — warm hit / selected count
- `#F7F8FC` — primary copy
- `#9AA0AD` — secondary copy

## Typography

- Archivo Black — campaign line and DUMBER wordmark; tightly tracked, large, blunt
- IBM Plex Mono — timecodes, counters, technical labels
- Reconstruct X typography and density with system UI fonts; every post remains editable DOM text

## Motion

- One dominant axis: continuous downward scrolling with a gradual camera pull-in
- One persistent visual language: the orb’s point ribbon reappears as the selected-card spectrum, highlighted words, and wordmark underline
- Primary handoff: orb pulse → selected-card perimeter → highlighted words → wordmark underline
- Scroll begins calm, accelerates into selection, then becomes slow and syrupy under the end lockup
- The meme face appears briefly inside the flow; it never interrupts the feed with a standalone cutaway

## Composition

- A deterministic DOM-authored X page fills the frame edge to edge
- Keep the selected post readable; effects live at its perimeter and on 2–5 short phrases only
- Campaign copy appears only after the product payoff, over a still-moving feed
- Status UI stays left of X's Grok control

## What NOT to Do

- No isolated cold-open poster or black end-card void
- No `HIT HARDER`, wellness warning, moral lesson, or product explanation
- No full-screen meme interlude
- No sequence of unrelated cards, wipes, or presentation slides
- No rainbow treatment limited to a thin border; the light must visibly travel through the scene
- No loose connector path between the scanner and a post
- No captured webpage, screenshot layer, browser chrome, or personal feed data
